Data we collect

This is an informational website with no forms: we do not collect your name, email, or phone directly here. Contact happens through external channels you choose to start — WhatsApp, email, and Calendly.

To measure the site's performance and our campaigns, we use analytics and marketing cookies (via Google Tag Manager). They collect usage data in aggregate — such as pages viewed, device type, and traffic source.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts — the site's fonts are served by Google, which may log your IP address when delivering them.
  • Google Tag Manager, Analytics, and ad pixels — cookies and similar technologies to understand site usage and measure campaigns; data is handled under Google's and the ad platforms' policies.
  • WhatsApp, Calendly, and email — when you use these channels, your data is handled under each service's own privacy policy.

Your rights

You may request information about any data you've shared with us through our contact channels, as well as its correction or deletion.
